# Vagrant Rim

Vagrant Rim is a space shooter with a collection/build economy that helps the player progress through ship upgrades. The initial game aesthetics are going to be very geometry based e.g. The arcade version of Asteroids.

# General game play

Game play starts with the player docked at the mothership. The player will explore the galaxy in search of materials that help build both ship and mothership improvements. Loot will be found mostly in space wreckage. Space wreckage will be both existing and created via battle destruction. The mothership will not be piloted. It is "home base". We may move it around the map but not in a real time fashion like the players ship. The universe is basically unbound and distance will be constrained by fuel economy. FTL technology will allow the player save general fuel via transporting to FTL bouys. One emergency FTL will available to the player if they get stranded (i.e. they have fuel but not enough to get home) or upon ship destruction. This emergency FTL will only transport the player back to the mothership and all loot will be left at that location.

# Economy/Resources

I'd like to have to have three primary resources used to create FTL capacity and ship upgrades. Let's call them A, B, C for now and they should very from common to rare. Various combinations of resources will create different upgrades and FTL capacity. Fuel will be a separate resource retrieved from scrapped ships or unlimited quantities from the mothership.

# Tech stack
